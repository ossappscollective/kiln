# Code of Conduct

Kiln follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that address applies to every space of this project: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be kind and respectful. Welcome people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person, and assume good faith.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing others' private information
  are not acceptable.
- Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules,
  and may restrict participation of anyone who does.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ossappscollective/kiln/security/advisories/new)
marked "conduct", or by contacting an organization owner of
[ossappscollective](https://github.com/ossappscollective). Reports are handled in confidence, and
retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.
