Features

Everything between a merged pull request and a happy store page

Each feature below exists in the repository today. Anything that changes a store is behind its own switch, off by default.

Releases and review submission

Walk each version through prepare, submit, in review and release for App Store Connect and Google Play, including phased and staged rollouts.

Store writes are off until you enable them

Listing editing, diff and the pending cart

Edit titles, descriptions, keywords and release notes per locale. Every change waits in one pending cart where you see the diff of all of it before anything is published.

Graphics management

Pull the store's screenshots and icons back, compare them with your repository, add, replace or remove them, and stage the push like any other change.

ASO: keywords, competitors, rankings

Track keyword ranks and competitor listings from public store data. Read-only: no store credential is used and nothing is written.

Reviews inbox with AI summaries

Read reviews from both stores in one inbox. AI summaries and draft replies are opt-in, you choose per app what may be sent, and reviews are redacted first.

Public replies are off until you enable them

Insights and analytics

Installs, ratings and health, sales and subscriptions from both stores, plus RevenueCat revenue and MRR. A timeline of events next to the numbers, and period reports as Markdown or CSV.

Expo and EAS

Follow EAS builds, submissions and over-the-air updates. Install a build from a QR code or with Orbit.

CI workflows

Start a GitHub Actions workflow, follow runs from GitHub and EAS, and see which run produced which store build. Your CI builds and signs; Kiln orchestrates.

Price changes with safety gates

In-app purchase and subscription prices sit behind two switches, a dry run, step-up confirmation and a typed phrase in the cart.

Off by default

Alerts and webhooks

A rejected version, a broken credential, a falling rating or a failing sync reaches you in the inbox, by email, on the desktop, in Slack or Microsoft Teams, or as a signed webhook.

Roles, approvals and audit

Owner, admin, member and viewer roles, limited API tokens, two-factor and passkey confirmation for hard actions, admin approval of change sets, and an audit log of every write.

Desktop app and Docker

A desktop app for one person, or a container for a team, with SQLite or Postgres. The same code and the same data model either way.

How it works

Three steps, and you stay in control of the last one

  1. Connect your stores

    Add an App Store Connect API key and a Google Play service account. Each in-app guide shows the exact role the key needs, and "test connection" checks it. The keys go into the encrypted vault.

  2. Sync

    Kiln reads your apps, versions and listings. Reviews and numbers are read too, on the schedule you set. A sync never writes to a store, and keywords and competitors come from public store data.

  3. Stage and publish

    Prepare changes, collect them in the pending cart, review the diff, and confirm. Nothing reaches a store before that last step, and a person confirms it.

Security and privacy

Built for keys that can publish your apps

A store key can change what millions of people install. Kiln treats it that way.

An encrypted vault

Credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, never returned by the API and never logged. The master key lives in your environment, not in ours.

Nothing is sent early

Changes are staged first. Each kind of store write has its own switch, off by default, and a preview before it is applied.

Roles and an audit log

Four roles, tokens limited to the apps and actions you choose, and an audit log that records every write with who made it.

Step-up for hard actions

Publishing, submitting, rollouts and price changes can require a fresh passkey, password or authenticator code. Price changes also need a typed phrase.

AI is opt-in

Off until you set a provider and key. You see what each task sends, choose per app which reviews may be sent, and reviews are redacted first. Small local models work too.

No telemetry, no trackers

Kiln sends no usage data, and this site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics.

Run it on your own machine

Kiln needs one secret, KILN_MASTER_KEY, which encrypts your store credentials. Keep a copy: losing it means entering every credential again.
